This Privacy Policy ("Policy") explains how Cotikai ("the Developer") handles user information and data within the application Circum Snap ("the App"). 
By using the App, you are deemed to have agreed to this Policy.

## 1. Information We Collect
The App may collect the following types of information.<br>
### Information Automatically Collected Within the App<br>
The App may allow third-party services to collect the following information for advertising and app improvement purposes:
- Advertising ID
- App usage data
- Crash logs
- Device information

### Information Sent for Weather Data Retrieval <br>
The App uses the Open-Meteo API to retrieve weather information.
When requesting weather data, the following information may be transmitted to the Open-Meteo servers:
- User location information (latitude and longitude)
- or IP address<br>

This information is used solely for displaying weather data and is not used for any other purpose. <br>

## 2. Purpose of Use
Collected information is used for the following purposes:
- Displaying and optimizing advertisements
- Improving app quality and fixing bugs
- Enhancing features based on usage analysis

The Developer does not use collected information to identify individual users.

## 3. Third-Party Services
The App may use the following third-party service for advertising:
- Google AdMob (Google LLC)

These services may collect data necessary for delivering advertisements.<br>
For details, please refer to the privacy policies of each service.

## 4. Handling of Image Data
- The App does not transmit captured or edited images to external servers.
- Image data is processed only on the user's device and is not viewed or stored by the Developer or any third party.
- Rights to images captured or edited by the user belong to the user.

## 5. User Rights
Users may reset their advertising ID or disable personalized ads.
These settings can be changed from the device's system settings.

## 6. Changes to This Policy
The Developer may update this Policy as necessary.<br>
Revised versions will take effect when posted on the public webpage.
